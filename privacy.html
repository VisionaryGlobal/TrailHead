Privacy Policy

Last updated September 2026

JourneyCake is an offline audio player. This page explains, plainly, what happens to your data.

What JourneyCake does not do

  • It does not collect, transmit, or store any information on a server. There is no server.
  • It does not use analytics, trackers, or advertising SDKs of any kind.
  • It does not require an account, login, or personal information.
  • It does not access your contacts, location, camera, or microphone.

What stays on your device

Audio files you import, the playlists you create, and your weekly tracker check-ins are stored entirely in your device's local browser storage (IndexedDB). This data never leaves your device, is never uploaded anywhere, and is only accessible within the app itself.

Uninstalling

If you uninstall JourneyCake or clear your browser's site data for it, all imported audio, playlists, and tracker history are permanently deleted along with the app. There is no copy stored anywhere else to restore from.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the update will be posted on this same page with a new "last updated" date.

Contact

JourneyCake is developed by Visionary Global (VG). Questions about this policy can be directed to the developer via the contact listed on the app's store page.
